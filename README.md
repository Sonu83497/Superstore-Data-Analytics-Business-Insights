# Superstore Data Analytics & Business Insights

## Overview

**Superstore Data Analytics & Business Insights** is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on extracting meaningful business insights from Superstore transactional data.

The project performs a comprehensive analysis across **sales, profit, customers, products, categories, regions, discounts, shipping, and time-based trends**. More than **70 analytical and business questions** were investigated to understand performance, identify patterns, uncover opportunities, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence by:

- Understanding overall business performance
- Identifying key sales and profit drivers
- Evaluating customer and product performance
- Analyzing regional and category-level trends
- Understanding the impact of discounts on profitability
- Identifying loss-making areas
- Discovering growth opportunities
- Developing strategic business recommendations

---

## Project Scope

The analysis covers the following major business areas:

### Sales Performance
- Overall sales performance
- Sales trends over time
- Sales by category and sub-category
- Sales by region and state
- Sales by customer segment
- Top-performing products

### Profitability Analysis
- Overall profit performance
- Profit by category and sub-category
- Profit by region and state
- Product-level profitability
- Identification of loss-making products
- Profit margin analysis

### Customer Analysis
- Customer contribution to sales
- Customer contribution to profit
- Top customers
- Customer segment performance
- Customer purchasing patterns

### Product Analysis
- Best-selling products
- Most profitable products
- Least profitable products
- Product-level sales performance
- Category and sub-category comparison

### Regional Analysis
- Regional sales performance
- Regional profitability
- State-level performance
- High-performing and low-performing locations
- Geographic opportunities

### Discount Analysis
- Discount distribution
- Relationship between discounts and sales
- Relationship between discounts and profit
- Identification of high-discount, low-profit areas

### Shipping Analysis
- Shipping mode performance
- Orders by shipping mode
- Sales and profit by shipping mode
- Shipping-related business patterns

### Time-Series Analysis
- Yearly performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Monthly profit trends
- Growth patterns
- Seasonal business trends
